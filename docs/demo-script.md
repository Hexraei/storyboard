## Shot list + narration

### 0:00-0:20 - Hook (the problem)
[Screen: title card or the Storyboard homepage]

"Every visual story starts with a storyboard - and that's the wall. If you're
an indie creator pitching a scene, a teacher turning a lesson into something
visual, or a parent making a bedtime story come alive, you need panels, and
drawing them takes hours or money. Storyboard removes that wall: you type the
story, AI draws the comic."

### 0:20-0:40 - What it is
[Screen: homepage, hover the style picker]

"This is Storyboard, built at Banana Hacks 2026. You paste in a short story,
pick an art style - comic ink, watercolor, pixel art, noir - and it gives you
back a finished comic strip where the characters actually look like the same
characters in every panel."

### 0:40-1:50 - Live demo
[Screen: paste a story - use Example 2, the stray cat astronaut. Click
'Make my comic'. Narrate over the generation.]

"Here's a story about a stray cat that sneaks into a space agency and
accidentally launches itself to the moon. I hit go, and two things happen.
First, an LLM story editor reads the story and writes a comic script - it
splits it into panels and locks a visual design for each character: our gray
tabby gets one description that's reused in every single prompt. Then each
panel is drawn by a text-to-image model, one by one, in the style I picked.

[While panels generate: "Each panel takes about ten seconds."]

Watch the cat - same gray tabby in the launch seat, same cat planting the flag
on the moon, same cat coming home a hero. That consistency is the hard problem
in AI comics, and it's solved by the pipeline, not by luck."

### 1:50-2:20 - Export + how it works
[Screen: click 'Download comic (PNG)', show the exported image, then the
README pipeline diagram or the repo]

"When it's done, one click exports the whole strip as a comic page - captions,
gutters, halftone header - as a PNG you can share anywhere. Under the hood it's
a four-step pipeline: LLM story editor, prompt builder with the locked cast,
seeded image generation per panel with retries, and a canvas layout engine.
Everything runs in the browser on free-tier infrastructure."

### 2:20-2:45 - Why it matters
[Screen: live app or the exported comic]

"Image AI isn't a feature here - it is the product. Remove it and there's no
strip. And the use cases are real: indie creators storyboarding pitches,
teachers and parents visualizing stories for kids, small businesses making
comic-style content without an illustrator."

### 2:45-3:00 - Close
[Screen: homepage]

"Storyboard: type a story, get a comic. It's live right now, free to try - the
link is in the description. Built at Banana Hacks 2026 with AI assistance,
start to finish."
